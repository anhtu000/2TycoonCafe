## Short description

Run a cozy three-floor cafe staffed by cute animals: serve customers by hand, adopt rare helpers and earn five stars.

## Description

Open a tiny cafe and grow it into a five-star, three-floor favourite run by adorable animals.

- **Serve by hand, then hire help.** Tap the coffee machine, tap the customer, collect your coins. Hand-served customers tip more, and golden VIPs only accept service from you.
- **Adopt animal helpers.** Capybaras, shibas, red pandas, a rare axolotl and more: every helper has a rarity and a perk (works faster or earns bigger tips). The odds are shown, and duplicates level your helpers up.
- **Build up, floor by floor.** Start at the Coffee Corner, then open the Bakery and the Dessert Bar. Every floor brings new menu items, new customers and new upgrades.
- **Always a next goal.** Short tasks every minute or two, five big stars to earn, and milestone bonuses as your menu levels up.
- **Come back tomorrow.** After five stars, every day brings three new orders and a "today's special" that sells for double. A 7-day gift brings a Rare helper on day 3 and the Snow Fox, a helper you can only get from the gift, on day 7. Missing a day pauses your streak instead of resetting it.
- **Your helpers keep working while you are away**, and the Night Shift upgrade lets them work longer.

## What makes it different (for the Poki team)

- A cutaway cafe of three stacked floors that the player opens one by one, on one screen, in portrait or landscape.
- A real choice between tapping and automation: serving by hand pays more and VIPs need the player, while adopted animals take over the routine work.
- A collection layer: 11 animal helpers with rarities, perks and levels, with published odds and pity rules (no real-money purchases).
- A clear end goal (five stars, about 40 to 60 minutes for a first-time player) plus daily orders and a timed challenge mode for players who keep going.

## Controls

- Desktop: mouse to tap machines, customers and cards. ESC opens and closes the menu (pause).
- Mobile and tablet: tap.

## Update plan (roadmap)

1. New cafe branches after five stars (beach, snowy lodge, forest), each with its own look and menu items; helpers and decorations carry over.
2. Regular customers who visit daily and gift exclusive decorations as they warm up to the cafe.
3. A weekly seasonal event with an event menu item, costumed customers and seven milestone rewards.
